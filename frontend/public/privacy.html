Privacy

Last updated 3 September 2026.

Your account and library

The main Cerum site offers Firebase and original Supabase accounts. Your selected provider handles your email, password-based sign-in, display name and private library. Firebase accounts use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, with limits of 100 favourites, 30 recent mixes and 100 feedback entries. Original accounts keep their existing Supabase Authentication and database records. Accounts are not automatically linked, and passwords or private libraries are not copied between providers. Choose the service where you created your account. The separate Firebase Hosting edition offers only Firebase accounts. You can search and play without signing in.

Music services and analysis

While Firebase is primary, public catalogue data and measured acoustic features are downloaded and cached in your browser; recommendations run on your device. When Supabase is primary, song searches and public recommendation requests go to its hosted music service. Public requests do not include Firebase authentication tokens or private library preferences. If that service is unavailable, the browser engine can provide public music suggestions. Personal picks use only the selected account's library on your device. Previously unanalysed previews can be processed on your device; new fingerprints last for the page session. The browser catalogue currently has no lyrics dataset.

Third-party services

When local search is insufficient, search text is sent to Apple’s public catalogue service. Artwork and permitted preview samples are streamed from their providers, which receive your IP address and normal request information. Selecting a song starts preview playback and may continue into queued previews. Samples are limited to 30 seconds. A YouTube link opens only when you choose it. Browser fallback charts are dated snapshots, labelled in the interface.

Storage and availability

Authentication providers keep your sign-in session in browser storage. Cerum also remembers your account-service and genre preferences and caches public song metadata. Audio is not included in the persistent catalogue cache. Removing a favourite or clearing history updates the selected account service. If that service cannot sync, records are not silently saved with a different provider. This site does not enable advertising cookies, Google Analytics or web audio uploads. Availability remains subject to the hosting and account providers' free-plan limits.
