Privacy

Last updated 18 August 2026.

Account data

If you create an account, Cerum stores your email, profile name, favourites, recommendation history, and listening interactions in Supabase so your library can follow you between devices. You can use the public catalogue without signing in.

Music services

Searches and recommendations are processed by Cerum’s Supabase service. Artwork, song details, and preview audio may come from third-party catalogue services. Preview audio plays only after you press Preview, and YouTube opens only after you choose its link.

Audio uploads

The hosted website does not accept audio uploads. The optional desktop analyser processes permitted audio through the backend running on your computer and deletes the temporary file after analysis. Cerum does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.
