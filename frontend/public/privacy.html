Privacy: Firebase edition

Last updated 3 September 2026.

Your account and library

This separate edition uses Google Firebase Authentication for your email, password-based sign-in and display name. Cloud Firestore stores up to 100 favourites, your 30 latest recommendation runs and up to 100 recent feedback records. Library documents are private to your signed-in account. Removing a favourite or clearing history updates Firestore. Accounts and history from the original Supabase-backed Cerum site have not been imported. You can search and play without an account.

Music analysis on your device

The public music catalogue and measured acoustic features are downloaded and cached in your browser. Recommendations run on your device. Previously unanalysed previews can be processed on your device; their new fingerprints last for the page session. This edition does not currently include a lyrics dataset and does not send your searches to Supabase.

Third-party services

When local search is insufficient, your search text is sent directly to Apple’s public catalogue service. Artwork and permitted preview samples are streamed from their providers, which receive your IP address and normal request information. Selecting a song starts preview playback and may continue into queued previews. Samples are limited to 30 seconds. A YouTube link opens only when you choose it. The chart list is a dated snapshot, not a continuously updated live feed.

Browser storage

Firebase keeps your sign-in session in browser storage. Cerum caches public song metadata and remembers your genre preference. Audio is not included in the persistent catalogue cache. This edition does not use advertising cookies, Google Analytics or audio uploads. Hosting and library availability are subject to Firebase’s free-plan limits.
