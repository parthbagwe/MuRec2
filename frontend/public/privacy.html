Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

Your account and library

The main Cerum site offers Firebase and original Supabase accounts. Your selected provider handles your email, password-based sign-in, display name and private library. Firebase accounts use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, with limits of 100 favourites, 30 recent mixes and 100 feedback entries. Original accounts keep their existing Supabase Authentication and database records. Accounts are not automatically linked, and passwords or private libraries are not copied between providers. Choose the service where you created your account. The separate Firebase Hosting edition offers only Firebase accounts. You can search and play without signing in.

Music services and analysis

Public catalogue data, measured acoustic features and a compact learned audio index are downloaded and cached in your browser. Recommendations and private preference matching run on your device. Public search follows the configured service, with a browser fallback if unavailable; requests do not include the other account provider's authentication tokens or private library preferences. The browser catalogue currently has no lyrics dataset.

On-demand AI

When you select a playable preview that is not in the AI index, the main Vercel site can send up to 30 seconds of that public preview to Cerum's own audio-analysis endpoint. The endpoint processes it in memory and returns a sound fingerprint and predicted mood similarities; it does not save the raw audio or send your favourites, history, passwords or sign-in tokens to an AI provider. Your browser can store up to 24 derived analysis results, keyed by a hash of the preview URL and model version. A warm server instance can temporarily cache up to 64 derived results keyed by audio hash. The server uses short-lived, hashed network identifiers for instance-local rate limiting. The hosting provider receives ordinary request/network information under its own policies. No microphone recording or personal audio-file upload is enabled on the main site. If analysis fails, acoustic matching continues. Mood labels are model predictions, not measured facts or lyric analysis.

Third-party services

When local search is insufficient, search text is sent to Apple’s public catalogue service. Artwork and permitted preview samples are streamed from their providers, which receive your IP address and normal request information. Selecting a song starts preview playback and may continue into queued previews. Samples are limited to 30 seconds. A YouTube link opens only when you choose it. Browser fallback charts are dated snapshots, labelled in the interface.

Storage and availability

Authentication providers keep your sign-in session in browser storage. Cerum also remembers your account-service and genre preferences, explicit recommendation feedback, and public song metadata. Guest feedback stays on the device; signed-in feedback syncs with the original account provider. Audio is not included in the persistent catalogue or derived AI caches, although your browser can normally cache streamed media. Clear site storage to remove device-only fingerprints and feedback. Removing a favourite or clearing history updates the selected account service. If that service cannot sync, records are not silently saved with a different provider. This site does not enable advertising cookies or Google Analytics. Availability remains subject to hosting, inference and account-provider limits.
