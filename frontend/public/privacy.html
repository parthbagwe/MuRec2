Privacy

Last updated 17 August 2026.

Local processing

MuRec2 runs as a local development application. Uploaded audio is sent only to the backend running on your computer, processed in a temporary file, and deleted immediately after analysis.

External services

Song searches may be sent to Apple’s Search API when the local catalogue does not contain enough matches. Preview audio streams directly from Apple only after you press Preview. YouTube opens only after you choose its link.

Stored information

MuRec2 does not create user accounts, use advertising cookies, or run analytics. It does not retain uploaded audio or a personal listening history.
