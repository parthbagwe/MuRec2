Purpose

Cerum is a music-discovery project. Recommendations combine measured audio characteristics, optional learned sound fingerprints, predicted mood and your feedback. They may not reflect your taste; AI predictions and transition quality are not guaranteed.

Third-party content

Song metadata, artwork, and preview samples remain the property of their respective owners. Available samples are streamed for discovery and limited to 30 seconds. Public previews may be decoded and temporarily processed for acoustic or on-demand AI analysis; the AI service does not save raw audio. Only derived fingerprints are cached. YouTube and other catalogue links lead to third-party services governed by their own terms. Use only content you are permitted to process.

Accounts and uploads

You are responsible for activity on your account. The main site does not record microphones or upload personal audio files. The optional desktop analyser is separate. Service availability, analysis speed and preview availability depend on external providers and hosting limits.
